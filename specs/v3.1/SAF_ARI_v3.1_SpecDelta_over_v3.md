The §14 log exists to catch exactly this. **"Relevant to human–AI cognition" and "should be measured by SAF" are different predicates.** Each point is dispositioned so it cannot resurface as a "new" proposal.

## 0.1 — Governing-clause addendum (v3.1)

The v2/v3 principle stands (*evidence before elegance; reliability before complexity; sustainability before synergy; borrowed-and-validated before invented; benchmark before claims; honest limits before reach*). v3.1 adds one clause and one architectural concept, both aimed at the failure mode in §0.0f.

**New governing clause — Relevance is not scope.**
> A science being *relevant* to human–AI cognition does not make it SAF's to *measure*. SAF is one instrument in a larger research ecosystem: a validated, reproducible, individual-level psychometric measure of the synergy–sustainability tension. Its value to that ecosystem scales with how well it does its specific job, not with how many adjacent concerns it absorbs. Vision at rung ASPIRATIONAL must not pull implementation at rung DESIGNED.

**New architectural concept — the Population-Observatory Layer (the home for relevant-but-out-of-scope concerns).**
> Macro, civilizational, sociological, and biological questions — cross-generational co-evolution, second/third-order societal effects, neuroplasticity validation — are **real and important**, and SAF *contributes the individual-level data they require*. They belong to a distinct **population-observatory layer** that *consumes* SAF's outputs (epidemiological cohorts, longitudinal neuroimaging, intergenerational designs), **not** to the instrument itself. Naming this layer gives those concerns a legitimate home without absorbing them into a psychometric tool that cannot answer them. SAF *feeds* the observatory; SAF *is not* the observatory.

# v3.1 ADOPTED CHANGES (expanded)

## A-3.1 · [V3.1-A] Brier score as a metacognitive-calibration field `[v3.1]`

**[DESIGNED]** Add a **strictly proper scoring rule** to the metacognitive calibration gap. Where the user emits probabilistic self-assessments (confidence in an AI output, in their own answer, in a verification), compute the **Brier score** = mean squared difference between the stated probability and the realized outcome (lower = better). Because it is strictly proper, it rewards *honest* probabilistic self-assessment and penalizes **both** over- and under-confidence — sharper than the existing calibration slope (§5.8d).

- **Where it feeds:** CA-08 (Metacognitive Self-Monitoring) evidence and the calibration-gap metric, **alongside** (not replacing) the calibration slope.
- **Freeze:** a new *field* on an existing neuron — permitted. The self-rating widget continues to feed **only** the calibration gap, **never** the behavioral scores (unchanged design rule).
- **Caveat:** Brier requires a realized outcome to score against; when no outcome is observable in-session, the field returns N/A (never zero).

## B-3.1 · [V3.1-B] Causal DAG pre-registration `[v3.1]`

**[DESIGNED — estimation data-gated]** This is the one genuinely non-redundant gap the proposal surfaced. The fix is **pre-registration, not premature fitting.**

- **Freeze the hypothesis now:** a structural causal hypothesis whose **nodes are existing** dimensions, 4D CSPC states, and sustainability observables (λ, retention stability), and whose **edges are the hypothesized causal directions** already sketched in the framework's causal order (AL upstream → orchestration/agency gating the generative dimensions → EC+CS protecting against the explanation trap → the longitudinal-sink dimensions → future θ / transfer). Recorded as a frozen artifact so the eventual analysis is **confirmatory**.
- **Name the estimation toolbox (do not run it):** structural causal models; potential outcomes; **longitudinal g-methods** for time-varying confounding (stakes, model-era nonstationarity, selection); dynamic SEM; Bayesian online change-point detection for model-era shifts. The **counterfactual probes** (commitment 9, "manufacture the counterfactual") are the data-collection primitive that makes these estimable; the partner-reliability map becomes a causal moderator.
- **Sharpening of §8.3:** the predictive-validity gate stays association-based for now; the causal claim ("behavior X *causes* +Δ stability") is a *future* upgrade gated on the corpus, not a substitute for the gate.
- **Hard rule:** **no causal-discovery fitting on the 26-chat pilot corpus** (§14.1). PC/FCI/GES on a single-archetype pilot produce misleading DAGs that would corrupt the pre-registered bets.

## C-3.1 · [V3.1-C] Iatrogenic-risk disclosure gate `[v3.1]`

**[DESIGNED]** Measurement and *disclosure* are different acts with different ethics. Summative disclosure of a negative sustainability signal (erosion, cognitive debt, low autonomy) is gated:

- **Permitted only when all hold:** the recipient is an **adult**; **consented**; the disclosure is **formatively framed** with an **actionable next step**; it is **never a bare negative verdict** ("your autonomy is eroding" with nothing else); and there is **distress-routing** — any signal of distress suppresses summative disclosure and routes to support resources.
- **Minors and low-validation tiers: formative-only** — hardening the existing formative/ecology commitment (v3 §9). No erosion/debt verdict is shown to a minor.
- **Ties to V3.1-F:** the A/B study measures whether disclosure itself changes behavior (theater) or wellbeing (iatrogenic harm), informing the gate's thresholds.

## D-3.1 · [V3.1-D] Relational-AI-psychology grounding `[v3.1]`

**[DESIGNED]** Attachment, anthropomorphism, and parasocial-bond formation with AI are under-tracked and show up behaviorally in transcripts. v3.1 documents them as the **theoretical mechanism and evidence source for existing CA neurons** — **not** a new construct:

- over-deference / failing to correct AI errors → **CA-15** (AI Sycophancy Resistance, inverse signal);
- anthropomorphic attribution / treating the AI as a social agent → **CA-06** (Adaptive Trust Calibration), **CA-13** (Identity Authorship Preservation);
- resistance to overriding the AI → **CA-01** (Sovereign Override Capacity).
- **Open question (flagged, not silently decided):** *task-irrelevant personal disclosure* may not map cleanly to any existing neuron. It is surfaced for human review rather than forced onto one — if it has no clean home, it stays an unscored observation, not a new neuron.

## E-3.1 · [V3.1-E] Behavioral-economics grounding `[v3.1]`

**[DESIGNED]** Documentation/grounding only:
- **Present bias** (immediate AI ease vs. long-term debt) = a restatement of the founding synergy/sustainability thesis — already the engine.
- **Prospect theory / loss aversion** (the pain of wrong self-reliance felt more than the pleasure of correct self-reliance) = the theoretical motivation for **AUI-08** (Dependency Risk Awareness), **CA-01** (Sovereign Override Capacity), and the **EC** dimension. The theory *explains*; the neurons *measure*. No new construct.

## F-3.1 · [V3.1-F] A/B disclosure-effects study pre-registration `[v3.1]`

**[DESIGNED — analysis data-gated]** Pre-register (OSF, per v3 V14) a randomized study assigning users to different score-disclosure conditions and logging downstream behavior + wellbeing. Pairs with the **theater-rate (TR)** dashboard as the continuous Goodhart monitor. Pre-register **null results** as publishable. Analysis runs when the corpus and consent infrastructure exist.

## The throughline

The proposal pointed a telescope at the stars — co-evolution, causality, neuroscience, society — and most of what it saw is real. But the foundation the telescope stands on is still 26 chats. Those are different problems, and **solving the civilizational one does not get easier by weakening the foundation-building.** v3.1 keeps the foundation honest: it adopts the four freeze-compliant grounding/instrument upgrades that genuinely strengthen the measure, pre-registers the one real causal gap instead of fabricating an answer, builds a wall (the iatrogenic gate) where disclosure could harm, names a home (the observatory layer) for the concerns that are relevant-but-not-ours, and writes the scope-discipline clause that lets the next wave of "essential dimensions" be triaged on sight. *Relevance is not scope. Evidence before elegance.*
