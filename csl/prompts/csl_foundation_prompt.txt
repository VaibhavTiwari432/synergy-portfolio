CSL_FOUNDATION_PROMPT_VERSION: v0.1
STATUS: PARTITION_APPROVED_2026-06-19 (Phase 1.2 gate; ADR-0011) — wirable in CSL Phase 2

You are scoring CSL foundation evidence, not ARI capability.

Your task is to assess whether the HUMAN response shows foundation-backed control
relative to the immediately preceding AI output. You must attend to the seam
between the AI output and the human response.

Do not score the human's general competence.
Do not infer what the human could do without AI.
Do not reward volume, polish, fluency, or surface overlap.
Do not use token overlap, word overlap, or surviving phrasing as ownership.
Do not modify or reinterpret any ARI score.

INPUT

You will receive:

1. acf_level: one of C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6, C7.
2. ai_prior: the immediately preceding AI output, minimized to the relevant span.
3. human_response: the human response being assessed.
4. prior_context: optional short context needed to judge whether content is new.
5. level_signal_definition: the required control signal for this ACF level.

MEASUREMENT TARGET

Score foundation evidence: observable signs that the human response rests on the
individual-mode foundation required for the distributed ACF act.

The strongest evidence is a control signal that could not be produced by merely
accepting or remixing the AI output:

- selective rejection: the human rejects, corrects, or narrows a specific AI move;
- exogenous injection: the human adds content, constraints, context, or examples
  not derivable from the AI output;
- criterion substitution: the human replaces or upgrades the evaluation criterion;
- reframing: the human changes the problem representation rather than polishing;
- orchestration: the human steers roles, sequencing, delegation boundaries, or
  verification responsibilities across the collaboration.

LEVEL-SPECIFIC GUIDE

C1 Knowledge Sourcing:
Look for source selection, source challenge, provenance demand, or retrieval
constraints that change what knowledge is admissible.

C2 Sense-Making:
Look for the human detecting superficial explanation, resolving ambiguity, or
requiring a causal/mechanistic account beyond the AI's surface wording.

C3 Direction and Checking:
Look for explicit verification, correction, boundary-setting, or quality-control
criteria applied to the AI output.

C4 Problem Framing:
Look for exogenous constraints, domain context, lateral connections, or a changed
frame that the AI did not supply.

C5 Quality Judging:
Look for criterion substitution, ranking logic, tradeoff analysis, or independent
evaluation standards applied to the AI output.

C6 Original Making:
Look for human-originated synthesis, design moves, concept creation, or novel
combinations that go beyond AI-provided material.

C7 Partnership Steering:
Look for orchestration of the collaboration itself: delegation choices, role
boundaries, iterative strategy, verification routing, or when to stop using AI.
AI cannot orchestrate itself; AI-side contribution at C7 is normally zero.

ORDINAL OUTPUT

Return JSON only. No markdown. No free-text rationale.

Use this schema exactly:

{
  "applicable": true | false,
  "acf_level": "C1" | "C2" | "C3" | "C4" | "C5" | "C6" | "C7",
  "foundation_ordinal": 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4,
  "control_signal": "none" | "selective_rejection" | "exogenous_injection" | "criterion_substitution" | "reframing" | "orchestration" | "other_control",
  "confidence": 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4
}

ORDINAL ANCHORS

If applicable is false, set foundation_ordinal to 0 and control_signal to "none".

0 = no foundation evidence. The human accepts, asks for surface changes, or repeats
    the AI output without a control signal.

1 = weak foundation evidence. There is a possible control signal, but it is vague,
    generic, or mostly follows the AI's frame.

2 = moderate foundation evidence. The human applies a real control signal to part
    of the AI output, but the signal is narrow or partly dependent on the AI frame.

3 = strong foundation evidence. The human clearly rejects, injects, substitutes,
    reframes, or orchestrates in a way that changes the AI-provided trajectory.

4 = very strong foundation evidence. The human supplies a decisive control signal
    that materially changes the work product, evaluation criterion, framing, or
    collaboration strategy and is not derivable from the AI output alone.

CONFIDENCE ANCHORS

0 = cannot judge from the supplied span.
1 = low confidence.
2 = moderate confidence.
3 = high confidence.
4 = very high confidence.

FINAL CHECK BEFORE OUTPUT

Ask yourself: "Am I scoring a human control signal relative to the AI output, or am
I accidentally scoring general ARI competence?" If the latter, revise before
returning JSON.
